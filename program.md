# Band Program (4 days/week)

Schedule: Mon Torso A · Tue Limbs A · Wed rest · Thu Torso B · Fri Limbs B · Sat–Sun rest

Sets/reps: 2–3 sets per exercise. Take each set to failure or within 2 reps of failure.
Aim for roughly 8–20 reps. If you can do more than 20 clean reps, use a heavier band,
combine bands (e.g. Med+Heavy), or step farther from the anchor. Rest 60–120 sec.
Log the band and reps each set and try to beat last time (more reps or a heavier band).

Back safety (hinge/deadlift moves): brace your core, keep a neutral spine, hinge only as
far as comfortable, go light while the back is sore, and stop if pain is sharp or runs
down the leg.

## Torso A (push) — Monday
1. Band incline chest press — anchor low behind you; press up and forward at ~30°. (replaces Smith incline bench)
2. Band lat pulldown — anchor high, kneel facing it; pull elbows down to your ribs. (lat pulldown)
3. Band-resisted push-up — band across upper back, ends pinned under hands. (dips)
4. Band wide-grip seated row — anchor at chest height or band around feet; elbows flared. (wide cable row)
5. Band chest fly — anchor mid-height behind; hug-a-tree arc, slight elbow bend. (pec deck)
6. Band lateral raise — stand on band; raise arms out to shoulder height. (lateral raises)

## Limbs A (legs) — Tuesday
1. Band seated calf raise — band looped around ball of foot; point toes into it. (seated calf raise)
2. Band leg extension — anchor low behind chair, band around ankle; straighten the knee. (leg extension)
3. Band front squat — stand on band, handles at shoulders; sit straight down, chest up. (hack squat)
4. Band hip thrust / glute bridge — band across hips, held down by hands or anchored; drive hips up. (hip thrust)
   Back: lift with the glutes, ribs down, don't arch the low back at the top.
5. Band single-arm tricep pushdown — anchor high; elbow pinned at side, push to straight arm. (single-arm pushdown)
6. Band biceps curl — stand on band; elbows at sides, curl up. (DB curl)

## Torso B (pull) — Thursday
1. Band bent-over row — stand on band, flat back; hinge only as far as comfortable, row to belly. (barbell row)
   Back: brace, neutral spine, stop if pain is sharp or runs down the leg.
2. Band overhead press — stand on band; press handles straight overhead. (shoulder press)
3. Band single-arm row — anchor mid-height or stand on band; elbow to hip. (DB row)
4. Band chest press — anchor mid-height behind you, standing; press straight forward. (DB bench)
5. Band upright row — stand on band; elbows to shoulder height. Stop if shoulders pinch. (upright row)
6. Band deadlift — stand on band; hips back, neutral spine, stand tall. Go light while back is sore. (rack pull)
   Back: brace, neutral spine, stop if pain is sharp or runs down the leg.

## Limbs B (arms) — Friday
1. Band standing calf raise — stand on band, handles at sides; rise high on toes. (standing calf raise)
2. Band close-grip chest press — anchor behind; hands close, elbows tucked. (close-grip bench)
3. Band drag curl — stand on band; drag hands up your body, elbows back. (drag curl)
4. Band overhead tricep extension — stand on band or anchor low, face away; extend overhead. (overhead cable extension)
5. Band single-arm concentration curl — seated, stand on band, elbow braced on inner thigh. (single-arm preacher curl)
6. Band split squat — front foot on band; rear foot elevated if comfortable; drop straight down. (Bulgarian split squat)
7. Band Romanian deadlift — stand on band, soft knees; push hips back, neutral spine. Light while back is sore. (RDL)
   Back: brace, neutral spine, stop if pain is sharp or runs down the leg.
